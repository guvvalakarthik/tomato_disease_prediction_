Model card

Status: validation pending. The live compatibility model is legacy release 0.1.0 and is not field-ready or calibrated.

Intended use

Educational screening of a clear photograph containing one tomato leaf across nine disease or pest classes and healthy. It is not a definitive diagnosis or treatment prescription.

Release design

The planned validated release uses MobileNetV3-Small, leaf-group-aware splits, temperature scaling, OOD rejection, ONNX CPU inference, and class-activation maps.

Required evidence

Per-class clean metrics, confusion matrices, calibration, locked OOD rejection, expert-reviewed local field evaluation, artifact parity, CPU latency, and user-study findings must be published before field-ready wording is used.

Limitations

Controlled-background data can inflate accuracy. Similar symptoms have different causes. Unsupported crops, novel conditions, multiple diseases, early symptoms, blur, lighting, and occlusion can produce errors or uncertain results.
